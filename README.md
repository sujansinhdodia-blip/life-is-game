## ✨ Features

- 💌 **Intro Gatekeeper**: Emotional entrance with music autoplay handling.
- 📜 **Cinematic Typewriter**: Smooth typewriter sequences with thoughtful pacing.
- ⏳ **Interactive Story Timeline**: Scroll-triggered moments with gentle reveal animations.
- 📸 **Polaroid Memory Gallery & Lightbox**: Dynamic image loader with lightbox and keyboard navigation.
- 📖 **The Letter**: Handcrafted parchment glassmorphism card with heartfelt words.
- 🎴 **Why I Love You Cards**: 3D interactive flip cards with bespoke icons and glow accents.
- 💍 **The Proposal**: Dignified question with soft celebratory or comforting outcomes.
- 🌸 **Falling Petals & Ambient Sparkles**: Custom Canvas particle system and interactive sparkle cursor.
- 🎵 **Floating Music Player**: Discreet audio controller with volume and mute toggles.

## 🔍 SEO & Performance Optimization

This project is fully optimized for search engines and social sharing:

- **Semantic HTML5**: Strict heading hierarchy (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>`), semantic sections (`<main>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`), and accessible ARIA attributes.
- **OpenGraph & Twitter Cards**: Dynamic social sharing cards with high-resolution preview images.
- **JSON-LD Structured Data**: Schema.org `WebSite` metadata embedded for search rich snippets.
- **Automated Sitemap & Robots**: Generated dynamically at `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`.
- **Fast Performance**: Next.js App Router with image optimization, preconnected Google Fonts, and lightweight bundle footprint.
